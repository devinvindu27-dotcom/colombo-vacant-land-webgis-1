Vacant Land Planning Information

This Web GIS supports urban planning by identifying vacant land locations, comparing parcel sizes, understanding population distribution and examining access to transport infrastructure. These datasets can help planners identify areas for further investigation and potential urban redevelopment.

Note: Redevelopment suitability requires a separate spatial analysis. Vacant land alone does not establish development permission or land ownership.
